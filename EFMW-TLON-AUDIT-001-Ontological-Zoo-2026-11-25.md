## Scope and method
This report expands the previously published EFMW-TLON-002 documentary assessment into a 23-operation ledger. Criteria are retrospective and deliberately bounded; no new experiments, independent reviewer approval, corpus-wide scan, or executable Zoo run is claimed. PASS means the specific documentary criterion is supported, not that the whole framework passes. FAIL applies only to a specified contradicted criterion. UNRESOLVED means missing evidence or execution. NOT_APPLICABLE requires documented exclusion.

## Separate documentary strengths
Negative-result preservation, explicit rejection conditions in selected experiments, and separation of formal and physical evidence are supported by the dissertation, ROT-009 and known-zero report. ROT-009 excludes the frozen source only. The known-zero calibration failure is not a failure of the entire research program. The grid result narrows a prior comparative claim.

## Simulation boundary
EFMW-TLON-002 evaluated 560 hypothetical configurations; 204 had zero final backlog and 232 reached risk >=0.75. The general simulation showed elevated risk even without new mismatch or explicit gain. These are model outputs, not institutional observations. Actual Tlonification risk is UNRESOLVED.

## Correction order
1. Freeze a deduplicated claim inventory and source dependency graph.
2. Measure claim arrivals and independent resolutions over fixed windows.
3. Repair known-zero calibration on fresh held-out trials; isolate signed residual versus memory length.
4. Establish independent challenge and legitimate reversal authority.
5. Calibrate separate backlog, unsupported adoption, divergence and correction-failure metrics before assigning a regime.

## Verdict
UNRESOLVED at program level. Evidence of corrective activity exists; adequacy and physical validity remain unestablished. This report preserves adverse findings rather than averaging them into an overall score.
