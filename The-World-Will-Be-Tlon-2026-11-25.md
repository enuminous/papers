# The World Will Be Tlön
## Artificial Intelligence, Hyperstition, and the Ontological Singularity

Matthew Chenoweth Wright · Monolithic LLC · with AI assistance · November 25, 2026

*Condensed publication edition of the originating essay.*

In Borges' 1940 story Tlön, Uqbar, Orbis Tertius, an invented civilization acquires a conceptual architecture so compelling that humanity begins to reorganize its understanding of reality around it. Reality need not be physically destroyed to be conceptually displaced.

A reported social media invocation of hyperstition by Sam Altman prompted the present essay; the post and any claim of first use remain unverified. Hyperstition concerns representations that contribute to their own material realization. Tlonification, as proposed here, concerns the displacement of independently supported knowledge by an adopted representational system.

Artificial intelligence can elaborate narratives, produce explanations, generate software and support institutional adoption at scale. A narrative can motivate an intervention; its consequences can then be treated as confirmation. Yet a self-fulfilling prediction does not necessarily establish its explanatory premises.

The proposed Ontological Singularity describes a regime in which consequential claim production and adoption persistently overwhelm independent correction. It is not a demonstrated event or a forecast. Its significance lies in the distinction between the power to construct representations and the ability to test them.

EFMW is not exempt. Recursive coherence may produce fruitful hypotheses or recursively reinforce mistakes. Formal proof, reproducible computation, independent criticism and physical measurement have different evidentiary roles. An accountable Principal must preserve uncertainty and accept legitimate correction, rather than simply make its institution's narrative more persuasive.

The Tlonify framework operationalizes these ideas in a preliminary dynamical model and proposed experimental program: https://github.com/enuminous/Tlonify. Its exploratory simulations reveal that a composite risk score can conflate verification overload with epistemic displacement, motivating a more discriminating diagnostic.

Imagination is not evidence. Repetition is not replication. Coherence is not truth.

The world will be Tlön—unless we preserve the right of reality to disagree.
