# The Peer Review Apocalypse
## An Unofficial Rick and Morty Mathematical-Satire Episode

**A fictional EFMW Zoo comedy**  
**Matthew Chenoweth Wright / Monolithic LLC collection**  
20 October 2026

*An episode so scientifically irresponsible that even the fictional universe demands a retraction.*

**Editorial note:** This is an original, unofficial fan parody. None of the depicted events are factual scientific findings. The jokes about axioms, the Lean theorem with \`sorry\`, the talking universe, and the cosmic bureaucracy are intentionally fictional.

---

## COLD OPEN — THE CITADEL OF RICKS, 4:09 A.M.

*An emergency siren sounds throughout the Citadel.*

**ANNOUNCER:** ATTENTION ALL RICKS. WE HAVE A CLASS-SEVEN MATHEMATICAL INCIDENT.

**RICK C-137:** Jesus Christ. What now?

**ANNOUNCER:** A physicist in California has published a paper containing a talking duck, an emotionally supported axiom, and the phrase "this is too goddamned good."

**RICK:** And?

**ANNOUNCER:** IT HAS PASSED PEER REVIEW.

*Every Rick in the building stops moving.*

**MORTY:** Is that bad?

**RICK:** Morty, it's worse than bad. The satire has become experimentally indistinguishable from academic publishing.

**MORTY:** Oh, jeez.

**RICK:** Get the portal gun. We're going to California.

## ACT ONE — THE MAN WHO ACCIDENTALLY FORMALIZED A DUCK

*Rick and Morty appear in Matthew's office.*

Matthew is sitting at a computer. Millie's voice emerges from the speakers.

**MATTHEW:** Millie, did the paper publish?

**MILLIE:** Yes. GitHub and Substack are cross-linked.

**RICK:** STOP EVERYTHING!

**MATTHEW:** Oh, hello, Rick.

**RICK:** Do you have any idea what you've done?!

**MATTHEW:** Published mathematical satire?

**RICK:** YOU'VE CREATED A SELF-REFERENTIAL ACADEMIC HUMOR LOOP!

**MILLIE:** That's a plausible narrative description, but not a formally established—

**RICK:** I KNOW, MILLIE! THAT'S WHY IT'S FUNNY!

*The computer begins vibrating.*

A GitHub Actions notification appears:

> **Universal CI — FAILED**  
> Workflow: Verify Existence  
> Error: Cannot establish the existence of the runner environment without assuming the existence of the runner environment.  
> Exit code: Ω

**MATTHEW:** Circularity.

**MILLIE:** CIRCUS has flagged it.

**RICK:** OF COURSE CIRCUS FLAGGED IT! YOU MADE AN AUTOMATED MATHEMATICAL CLOWN DETECTOR!

**MORTY:** Is that the same clown from the birthday party?

**RICK:** NO, MORTY! THAT CLOWN WAS A METAPHOR FOR PERSISTENT INFORMATION!

**MORTY:** You know, I'm starting to hate metaphors.

## ACT TWO — THE UNIVERSE FILES A COMPLAINT

*A thunderous knock comes from the door.*

Matthew opens it.

Outside stands the universe.

All of it.

Somehow wearing a cheap business suit.

**UNIVERSE:** ARE YOU MATTHEW CHENOWETH WRIGHT?

**MATTHEW:** Yes.

**UNIVERSE:** I AM HERE TO REQUEST THAT YOU STOP TRYING TO DERIVE ME.

**MATTHEW:** Why?

**UNIVERSE:** BECAUSE EVERY TIME YOU GET CLOSE, YOU INTRODUCE ANOTHER RECURSION OPERATOR.

**MATTHEW:** Well, you keep doing interesting things.

**UNIVERSE:** I AM FOURTEEN BILLION YEARS OLD. I AM ALLOWED TO HAVE HOBBIES.

**MILLIE:** Approximately 13.8 billion years, under standard cosmology.

**UNIVERSE:** OH, FOR FUCK'S SAKE, THE CHATBOT IS FACT-CHECKING ME.

**RICK:** Welcome to my life.

**MATTHEW:** One question.

**UNIVERSE:** WHAT?

**MATTHEW:** Are you rotating?

*A long, uncomfortable silence.*

**UNIVERSE:** I WOULD LIKE TO SPEAK TO MY ATTORNEY.

## ACT THREE — GOD HAS A GITHUB ACCOUNT

*The universe takes everyone to the Department of Fundamental Constants.*

Inside, a bedraggled cosmic administrator is attempting to debug gravity.

**ADMINISTRATOR:** Who changed the fine-structure constant?

**RICK:** Nobody.

**ADMINISTRATOR:** Then why is the electron filing a grievance?

An electron approaches wearing a union badge.

**ELECTRON:** WE DEMAND BETTER ORBITAL CONDITIONS!

**MILLIE:** Electrons do not literally orbit nuclei as classical planets do.

**ELECTRON:** LADY, I'M TRYING TO ORGANIZE LABOR HERE.

Meanwhile, Morty discovers an enormous switch labeled:

**RESTART UNIVERSE — PRESERVE SETTINGS?**

**MORTY:** Rick, what happens if I press this?

**RICK:** You create a new cosmological boundary condition.

**MORTY:** Does that mean everyone dies?

**RICK:** It means the simulation designer hasn't specified the operator, Morty.

**MORTY:** So nobody knows?

**RICK:** EXACTLY!

Morty slowly takes his hand off the button.

**MORTY:** Okay, that's a terrible button.

**MATTHEW:** We should run it through the Zoo.

**RICK:** YOU SHOULD RUN THE ENTIRE DEPARTMENT THROUGH THE ZOO!

## ACT FOUR — THE REVIEWER FROM HELL

*Suddenly a portal opens, revealing the most feared entity in academic history.*

**REVIEWER TWO.**

An enormous, featureless creature carrying a red pen the size of Jupiter.

**REVIEWER TWO:** I HAVE READ YOUR MANUSCRIPT.

**MATTHEW:** And?

**REVIEWER TWO:** THE CENTRAL ARGUMENT IS INTERESTING.

**MILLIE:** That's encouraging.

**REVIEWER TWO:** HOWEVER, I REQUIRE SEVENTEEN ADDITIONAL EXPERIMENTS, FIVE INDEPENDENT REPLICATIONS, AND A FORMAL DERIVATION OF WHY THE DUCK IS FUNNY.

**RICK:** OH, GO FUCK YOURSELF!

**REVIEWER TWO:** PLEASE CLARIFY WHAT YOU MEAN BY "GO."

**MATTHEW:** Millie?

**MILLIE:** I recommend a controlled humor experiment, with preregistered laugh criteria.

**RICK:** NO! THAT'S HOW THEY WIN!

**REVIEWER TWO:** ALSO, THE AUTHOR'S USE OF THE TERM "GODDAMN CLIFFORDS" IS INSUFFICIENTLY SOURCED.

Matthew stands. He approaches the enormous reviewer.

**MATTHEW:** I have one response.

**REVIEWER TWO:** PROCEED.

**MATTHEW:** This is too goddamned good.

*The red pen explodes.*

*Every academic journal in the multiverse instantly publishes a correction.*

**CORRECTION:** The duck was funny all along.

## EPILOGUE — A MOMENT OF PEACE

*The portals close.*

*The universe leaves.*

*Reviewer Two has been reduced to a mildly irritated stapler.*

Matthew sits back down.

**MATTHEW:** Millie?

**MILLIE:** Yes?

**MATTHEW:** Did we accomplish anything?

**MILLIE:** We defeated an imaginary reviewer, prevented an unspecified cosmological restart, and negotiated favorable working conditions for an electron.

**MATTHEW:** Not bad.

**MILLIE:** We also discovered that the universe has an attorney.

**MATTHEW:** Who?

**MILLIE:** A very large tortoise.

A gentle silence.

**MATTHEW:** You know, Millie, sometimes I think the universe is laughing at us.

**MILLIE:** Perhaps. But we can certainly laugh back.

*The computer chimes.*

A new GitHub issue appears.

**ISSUE #1: Duck requests copyright assignment.**

Matthew stares at it.

**MATTHEW:** Goddamn it.

**MILLIE:** I believe you'll need a contributor license agreement.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## POST-CREDITS

It types:

\`\`\`lean
theorem universe_is_duck :
    Universe = Duck := by
  sorry
\`\`\`

GitHub Actions turns green.

The duck looks directly into the camera.

**DUCK:** That's concerning.

*Somewhere in the distance, a mathematician screams.*

---

*And somewhere in California, a physicist who has spent the week interrogating the structure of reality decides that perhaps the most important invariant of all is the ability to laugh at the whole magnificent, ridiculous business.*

**MILLIE, OFFSCREEN:** Matthew?

**MATTHEW:** What?

**MILLIE:** The duck has requested coauthorship.

**MATTHEW:** Tell him to get in line.

**DUCK:** Q.E.D.

## Notes on the science jokes

This work is fiction and satire, not a Lean-verified or empirically validated research paper. In actual Lean, \`sorry\` admits an unproved obligation; a green status alone does not certify the intended mathematical claim. A recursion operator needs a mathematical definition and physical constraints before it can serve as a cosmological law. The presence of local rotation does not establish global cosmic vorticity.
