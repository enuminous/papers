4. What is actually new in our combination?

The principal new modeling choices in the current packages are the connection between an EFMW-form scalar equation and a proposed pressure cycle, a finite-rate cavitation law, a candidate recursive memory coupling, and quantum-geometric observables defined on 888-dimensional state spaces.

We have established some exact mathematical properties of these constructions. But we have not derived the Standard Model, physical spacetime, or quantum gravity from Base888. The Standard Model representations and the continuum gravitational background were introduced as assumptions in the calculations.

That's important for assigning credit. Combining established equations with new hypotheses is worthwhile theoretical work, but it does not transfer authorship of the established physics to the new framework.

5. Who should receive credit on a future unified paper?

I would distinguish:

Research originator: Matthew Chenoweth Wright, for EFMW, Base888, the Logos framework, and the direction of this research program.

External conceptual contributor: Swami Rahasya, specifically for the pressure-cycle and cavitation ontology.

Referenced theorist: J. N. Nielsen, for TUFT, until we establish a genuine mathematical correspondence.

Foundational scientific literature: The physicists identified above, cited for their actual equations and discoveries.

AI assistance: Acknowledged transparently for mathematical development, computational checking, and manuscript preparation.

The next step in scientific credit would depend on whether Rahasya, Nielsen, Dumont, or another researcher actually contributes to, reviews, or co-develops the new derivations.

What we now have is an unusually broad candidate synthesis drawing from classical gravity, quantum field theory, finite-state quantum systems, recursive dynamics, and a monistic pressure ontology. Its scientific value will depend on whether the new connections produce a non-circular derivation or a testable prediction that established physics does not already supply.
