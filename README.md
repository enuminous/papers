- **[EFMW-RP-001: Detecting Relaxation Without Identifying Its Origin](EFMW-RP-001-Bubble-Memory-Detection-and-Identifiability.md)** — 29 October 2026. Derives the exact Maxwell equivalence, documents numerical verification, and separates laboratory-frequency relaxation from untested pressure-cycle and Stoney-scale claims. [Code, data, and interactive laboratory](https://github.com/enuminous/Bubble-Dynamics-Test/tree/1f47d523a3f1e6fa2e732eb493f54d8c4658cac4).

Long-form science journalism on simulated Langevin oscillator measurements (30,000 paired samples), a conventional Gaussian mutual-information prediction, uncertainty and time-shifted nulls, the proposed real-world MEMS data follow-up, and why statistical information does not itself establish informational gravity.

- **[The Monolithic Twenty-Three: A Falsification-First Experimental Program for EFMW](MONOLITHIC-23-EFMW-Physical-Falsification-Program.md)** — 28 October 2026. A proposed, not-yet-executed paper specifying 23 tests: three foundational identity/consistency gates, eighteen public-observation comparisons, and two accessible laboratory analogues. Includes hypotheses, conventional controls, failure conditions, reproducibility, readiness states and source portals; no EFMW-specific physical detection is claimed.

- **[When the Universe Balanced the Books: Monolithic's ME-007 Experiment](When-The-Universe-Balanced-The-Books-ME007-TOE001.md)** — 28 October 2026. Long-form science journalism on TOE-001's conserved ME-007-style informational-stress candidate, the nonuniqueness of ordinary/informational tensor partitions, 31 Python tests, eight symbolic identities, and a flat-cosmology no-bounce restriction. Clearly separates conditional classical consistency from quantum-gravity unification or empirical discovery.

- [TOE-001: Covariant EFMW-Inspired Unified Action and Correspondence Test](TOE-001-Unified-Action-and-Correspondence-Test.md) — 28 October 2026. A concrete GR + assumed Standard Model + mixed two-scalar action, conditional on-shell stress-energy conservation, stable linear mass spectrum, causal memory reduction, two-mode response and 15/15 local Python tests. **Not a Theory of Everything:** historical EFMW tensor correspondence, quantum gravity, emergent SM content and independent experimental signals remain unresolved.

- [QNET-001: Dynamical Connections and Obstructions to 3+1-Dimensional Emergence](QNET-001-Dynamical-Connections-and-Dimensionality-Obstructions.md) · [Protocol, code, and results](QNET-001-artifacts/README.md) — 28 October 2026. Finite quantum-graph connection dynamics, exact static ground-state counterexamples, and a proof that the full ground energy is independent of local matter dimension for J ≥ 0. A bounded negative result, not a derivation of spacetime or quantum gravity.

- [RHC-001: An EFMW-Based Candidate Formalization of Rahasya's Pulsating-Substrate Pressure Cycle](RHC-001-EFMW-Rahasya-Pressure-Cycle-Formalization.md) — 27 October 2026. Mathematical consequences are distinguished from unverified quantum, gravity, Stoney-time, TUFT, and consciousness claims.


- [Monolithic Zoo Dissection of Romy Mondello’s Separable Jacobian Counterexample](Mondello-Separable-Jacobian-Full-Zoo-Audit.md) — 26 October 2026, binary certification edition. All 51 Zoo certifications fail for insufficient evidence or unresolved risks; the exact algebra checks pass and the written proof survives. Fresh local Lean verification remains unperformed.
