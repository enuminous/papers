# Analysis decisions

Exploratory secondary analysis, recorded locally after checking file structure, before fitting the outcome models. No public preregistration or prospective human sample.

Primary contrast: does B, the half-decay geometrically weighted absolute error magnitude, improve held-out prediction of raw confidence over a baseline with signed memory, squared-error memory, recent errors, prior ratings, and published Bayesian uncertainty measures? Prediction: confidence declines as B rises at matched controls. Add one linear B term to an otherwise flexible regression. Separate descriptive tests for the coherence ratio and for signed adjustment of the next response. No decay selection. Repeat the primary contrast in session 2 with session 1 weights, excluding the entire test participant from training in both sessions.

Use the authors' trial and block IDs. Initialize each memory at zero per block; require five prior observations. Shortest angular residual is outcome minus response (positive in the increasing-angle direction), matching the authors' convention; use their signed radian error directly. A rating concerns the current placement, before outcome feedback. Score correction as the shortest signed angle from prior to current response. Train on participants, using five deterministic balanced folds. Primary metric: mean squared error averaged per participant, then across participants; positive improvement means lower loss after adding B. Report paired percentile bootstrap intervals resampling held-out participants with fitted predictions fixed; intervals omit training uncertainty. Report the sign and scale of the fitted B slope. No claim of practical importance without an advance margin.

Check the exact rational matched histories separately; control last residual, residual multiset, signed and squared memories. Vary histories and mirror signs to challenge recency explanations. A controlled comparison of identical probes after assigned histories remains prospective.
