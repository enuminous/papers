\[

\varphi=(1+\sqrt5)/2
\]

**for every adjacent pair in the complete source-ordered contiguous run**, not for a favorable triple selected after the fact.

**Executed result: NO SOURCE SUPPORT.** No original ledger statement established a nonarbitrary map from EFMW variables to an ordered sequence of physical reservoir-mode frequencies. Therefore the full-run ratio condition was **not evaluable**; the audit did not manufacture a favorable assignment of modes. ME-075 and ME-076 mention a single recursive phase-rate symbol \(\omega_{\rm rec}\), not a physical indexed oscillator-bath spectrum.

If H1 had passed its *source-derivation* check, it would have specified **a conventional oscillator bath with a golden-ratio-spaced spectrum**. This nonpromotion rule applies in the very same interpretation as any claimed PASS, not as a remote limitation.

\qquad b_j=z_j\omega_j^2a_j^2>0,

again for every adjacent pair, with no post-hoc exponent, mode selection or fitted weight assignment.

**Executed result: NO SOURCE SUPPORT.** No original source statement identified the complete physical bath weights or supplied the required source-to-weight mapping. The exponent \(-2\) was an **authored speculative guess**, frozen in advance to prevent tuning, **not** a known source-derived relation. Positivity of a conventional spectral measure cannot establish that ratio.

If H2 had passed its *source-derivation* check, it too would have described **a conventionally realizable oscillator bath with constrained weights**. It would **not** identify a new gravitational force or distinguish EFMW from a conventional reservoir having the same spectrum.

**For both:** Absence of a physically defined mapping means there is no legitimate \(\omega_j\) or \(b_j\) sequence upon which to test exact full-run ratios. We report a **completed bounded failure of source support**, not a numeric ratio FAIL and not a universal impossibility theorem.

## 5. Conventional spectral containment remains the mathematical baseline

IST-001's finite bath produces

\[
\gamma(t)=\sum_{j=1}^{N}b_j\cos(\omega_jt),
\quad b_j\ge0,\quad\omega_j>0,
\]

which is represented by the nonnegative discrete measure

\[
d\rho(\omega)=\sum_j b_j\,\delta_{\omega_j}(d\omega).
\]

This is a **conventional** oscillator-reservoir representation. The nonnegative discrete weights make the finite-bath kernel a member of that established class; deriving a restriction on its spectrum would not by itself prove a new gravitational source. For a continuum reservoir, further convergence, thermodynamic and causal assumptions are required.

The stronger scientific claim would require both independently justified calibration and prospective discrimination from comparably constrained conventional models. This audit did **not** conduct either kind of physical measurement.
