## Anti-circularity

- Neither coupling nor mass was chosen to fit an observation.
- The numerical example was specified in the scope before calculation and is labeled an illustration.
- The original CM hypothesis was not made to pass by inserting an unprovided matter interaction or relaxation action.
- The zero-coupling member of C1 was retained, as were the negative gauge-kinetic domain and coherence blind spots.
- Agreement with a known model is reported as equivalence, not independent confirmation of EFMW.
- The published limit was translated, not recomputed from unavailable raw data.
- Exact agreement between algebra and code is not physical validation.
- No particular operator's hypothetical existence is inferred from its presence in an action.

## Verification results

The saved calculation reports:

- 35 dimensionless forward-model grid cases.
- Maximum probability difference between diagonalization formula and numerical matrix exponential: 8.33e-17.
- Maximum unitarity residual: 6.44e-15.
- Maximum difference between the weak-conversion formula and numerical Born-amplitude integration: 5.55e-17.
- Symbolic checks of the scalar source, gauge factor, Euler–Lagrange equations, dispersion determinant and rephasing.
- Independent SI-energy-density and direct-SI-amplitude checks.
- Zero coupling, either field off, and parallel polarization all give zero modeled regeneration.
- Doubling g multiplies weak-regime counts by 16; halving both fields multiplies them by 1/16.
- 401 mass-scan predictions retain their approximation-error estimates.

The matrix-exponential and integral checks are different calculations inside the same workflow, not an outside replication.

## Novelty and surviving claim

The surviving claim is narrow:

> Within the declared weak-field, relativistic, single-scalar restriction and apparatus assumptions, the C1 pair coefficient c_MW generates the standard scalar–photon optical response with g=4c_MW.

The rejected stronger claim is:

> This optical response uniquely establishes EFMW.

The missing theory-level ingredient is an independently justified restriction on its free parameters or linked observables that differs from the identical comparator. It has not been manufactured in this report.
