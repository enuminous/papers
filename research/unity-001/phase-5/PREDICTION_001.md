## Hypothesis

Suppose a future, independently derived and physically admissible EFMW information stress \(I_{\mu\nu}\) yields a stationary mass-equivalent density \(\rho_I=I^{00}/c^2\) in a specified weak-field regime, and ordinary matter \(\rho_b\) can be constrained independently.

The conditional hypothesis is that \(H_1:\kappa_I\ne0\) changes a measured gravitational observable compared with \(H_0:\kappa_I=0\) and with **all admissible conventional mass/measurement alternative models**, under identical information and error budgets.

\[
\Delta a_r(r)=-G\kappa_I M_I(<r)/r^2
\]

is **only a weak-field response template**, not a new derived EFMW equation. The mass profile \(M_I\), the admissibility of \(I_{\mu\nu}\), the value and dimensions of \(\kappa_I\), and the measurement map are not supplied by the frozen research corpus.

## Current executed finding

The frozen **dimensionless synthetic** screen found conditional information under one restricted nuisance family, near-zero practical sensitivity under a nearly matching conventional profile, and **exact non-identifiability** with an additional conventional matter component having the same radial shape. The latter is a strong null model and prevents promoting the toy result into a claim about observed gravity.
