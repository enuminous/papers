# QG-022 — Newtonian quantum matter and retained tensor geometry

26 November 2026 · Monolithic LLC · EFMW exploratory computation · Not peer reviewed.

**PASS_BOUNDED_REDUCED_HAMILTONIAN.** Two mobile distinguishable masses, a single reduced Newtonian interaction, and two quantized TT modes extracted from the QG-015 local lattice coexist in a finite self-adjoint Hamiltonian. All 15 primary programmed checks passed. A follow-up finer matter-grid run supports the qualitative result, but the resonant dynamics remain grid-sensitive. No full covariant matter–metric action, relativistic causal mediation, nonlinear quantum constraints, matched continuum limit or new EFMW prediction is established.

## Specification

Dimensionless units, hbar=1. Two one-dimensional hard-wall intervals of width 2 with centers separated by D, each mass M=5. Central differences for kinetic energy, Dirichlet boundaries. D=3,4,6; the minimum gap between supports is D-2. The Newtonian part is T_A+T_B-g/(D+x_B-x_A), g=G M^2. The initial state is the third single-particle kinetic eigenstate for A and the ground state for B, or one separate superposition of the first two eigenstates for A. Both retained field modes start in the bare vacuum. The coupling is present from t=0: these are nonequilibrium preparations, not dressed eigenstates.

The QG-015 staggered operators (exact pinned source included, blob 808971a79a16d7fe50d7d2c5af18a3d20ac053ec) act on a periodic 13^3 field lattice. Two real cosine standing modes at k=(0,0,12 pi/13) have plus and cross polarizations, omega=2 sin(6 pi/13)=1.985417748196108. Both are normalized; the rank-four linear constraints and the free-mode stiffness are checked. This momentum is near the lattice cutoff, selected to approach the bare matter transition, not the infrared relativistic limit. The other modes, including sine partners and the homogeneous sector, are omitted. This is a two-mode truncation, not propagation through a fully dynamical 13^3 field.

Two fixed, spatially disjoint engineered stress envelopes S_A,S_B are generated by (K^2+L_0 K)/2 applied to localized tensor seeds, normalized, using K=QG-015 stiffness and L_0=-lap. The filter is the QG-020 construction. Their lattice centers are separated by six lattice cells, regardless of the Newtonian separation D. The envelope coefficient is q(x)=x^2 minus its single-particle ground-state expectation. It is a changing quadrupole-like operator, not the full physical stress-energy tensor of the moving masses. The projections onto the two modes, rescaled by the largest absolute projection, are C_A=(1,.35355339059327384), C_B=(.6501031274088436,-.7661538831022928). Set F_lambda=lambda(C_A,lambda q_A+C_B,lambda q_B). The scalar g and tensor lambda are independently chosen, without common gravitational matching. The source envelopes are not transported with the particles. Scalar free-space potential and tensor periodic cavity boundary conditions have not been matched.

H=H_m+H_g+H_int, H_g=omega sum a^dag a, H_int=sum F_lambda(a+a^dag). No independent Newtonian oscillator is added. The potential is the usual leading reduced scalar interaction; it does not follow here from solving the full operator ADM constraints on the same lattice. The field is represented by two independent number bases, cut=4,6,8. Finite matrices are Hermitian; canonical oscillator relations hold only away from the cutoff. Exact sparse exponential propagation, 61 times t=0..12 per trajectory. Negativity is the sum of negative eigenvalue magnitudes of the partially transposed reduced A-B state (mixed after tracing the field); field linear entropy is 1-Tr rho_g^2. No independent classical-mediator comparator is claimed in this stage.

## Conservation and constraint scope

On the parent tensor lattice, the two engineered spatial stresses separately have zero divergence and trace, even when multiplied by time-dependent matter operators. A bare seed has nonzero divergence. The TT modes have zero vacuum scalar curvature and divergence. This is not a demonstration that the full dynamical T^{mu nu} is conserved. The matter density on each hard-wall chain obeys exact discrete probability/number continuity: d p_i/dt=J_{i-1}-J_i, J_i=-2 Im(T_{i,i+1} sum psi_i^* psi_{i+1}), with zero boundary currents. The independent RHS is checked. Wall reactions and full momentum/energy stress, scalar and vector operator constraints, and GR Ward identities are not included. Energy conservation follows from the common time-independent Hamiltonian. Each sector's power is 2 Im <O psi,H psi>; the sum is zero. Finite-difference power errors are separately reported.

## Counting interactions once

For separated compact spheres, the Newtonian energy is (1/2) int rho Phi; the cross terms are equal, so their sum with the half factor yields -g/D once, not twice. Independent Gauss-Legendre quadrature over a homogeneous sphere of radius .2 in the external field of the other verifies this at five distances, max error 2.44e-15. Constant self energies are omitted. This does not solve a dynamical scalar constraint.

For frozen commuting F, completing the oscillator square gives an induced vacuum shift -sum F^2/omega. Direct 32-level diagonalization checks three coupling vectors with maximum discrepancy 1.11e-16. Including that shift again while retaining the explicit oscillator doubles the frozen energy correction. For moving matter [F,H_m] need not vanish: this identity is not an exact integration-out prescription for the moving system. The double_scalar and double_TT arms deliberately add the redundant scalar term or frozen shift to the full Hamiltonian; the resulting trajectories differ. Neither baseline nor these controls imply a fully matched relativistic EFT.
