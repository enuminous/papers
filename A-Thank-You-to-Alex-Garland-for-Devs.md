# A Thank-You to Alex Garland for *Devs*

**To: Alex Garland**  
**From: Matthew Chenoweth Wright, Monolithic LLC**  
**Date: 23 October 2026**  
**Subject: Thank you for *Devs***

Dear Alex,

Thank you for *Devs*. Your series became a starting point for a research project of mine, and I wanted to acknowledge that directly.

What stayed with me was the human question beneath the machinery: what happens to choice, responsibility, and grief when people believe the future is already known? You gave those questions emotional weight.

That helped inspire **Devs 2.0**, my work on intelligence, prediction, and human agency. Its central idea is bounded foresight: systems that describe possible outcomes, expose their assumptions, preserve the sources behind their claims, and revise their conclusions as evidence changes. Human beings retain the authority to decide what to do.

The project now includes a published paper, formal mathematical models, executable software, and a draft experiment to test whether explicit provenance and structured criticism improve the reliability of answers.

Whatever that research ultimately finds, the inspiration deserves clear credit. Your work gave me a question worth pursuing—and a memorable way to explain why it matters.

I thought you should know that *Devs* has had this consequence beyond the screen.

With thanks and respect,

**Matthew Chenoweth Wright**  
Monolithic LLC
