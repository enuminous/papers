## 2. Definition of "novel physical-law candidate"

Include an item when (a) there is a documented proposed regularity about physical reality, (b) it purports to go beyond a mere naming convention or existing standard identity, and (c) it could in principle be given independently measurable variables and falsifiable predictions. **Inclusion does not certify that any prediction is presently specified.**

Exclude from the law count: mathematical identities, purely finite combinatorial structural theorems, simulation algorithms, literary metaphors, commercial strategy, ethical norms, purely mathematical open-problem claims, and already established physical laws restated in EFMW language.

Where the same physical thesis appears in the 102 catalogue, historical axioms, papers and specialized repositories, it gets **one family ID**. Variants may later split after their physical definitions and contradictory predictions are compared.

### Evidence states

- **HISTORICAL-ASSERTION:** recorded claim only.
- **FORMALLY-SPECIFIED:** an explicit model can be analyzed; not proof of nature.
- **MATHEMATICALLY-CONDITIONAL:** a derivation is valid subject to supplied premises, often reproducing conventional physics.
- **NUMERIC-TOY:** an executed model or synthetic experiment; no external physical validation implied.
- **EMPIRICAL-OPEN:** distinctive observable/experimental comparison absent, incomplete, or unreproduced.
- **NOT-NOVEL-AS-STATED:** reduces to established structure in the tested specialization.
- **RETRACT / SUPERSEDE:** contradicted by source audit or overclaims explicitly corrected.

Physical-law candidates usually need both an equation **and** a nontrivial empirically distinguishable consequence to progress beyond an exploratory specification.

## 5. Historical extraction and coverage queue

The [curated claims catalogue](https://github.com/enuminous/EFMW-Scientific-Claims/blob/main/SCIENTIFIC_CLAIMS.md) covers writings from 2016–August 2025 in \`enuminous/medium\` and \`enuminous/medium2\`, describes ~1.6 million words and **1,158 keyword-extracted claim-bearing headings**. The extraction includes known science, rhetorical titles, duplicate phrases and satire. None of those rows is automatically a novel physical-law candidate. The historical catalogue also pre-dates several October 2026 developments.

**What remains to make the register genuinely portfolio-wide in the exhaustive sense:**

1. Inventory each current \`enuminous\` repository by full commit SHA and identify all claim-bearing files, not only READMEs. The historical lifecycle audit inspected 119 public repositories on 16 October 2026; this is not a current independently recounted total.
2. Normalize the 1,158 archival headings against their source paragraphs and equations. Mark \`duplicate\`, \`physical-law candidate\`, \`standard theory\`, \`mathematical only\`, \`engineering only\`, \`metaphor/satire\`, or \`insufficient information\`.
3. Compare all source-level Monolithic 102 entries against NPL IDs, particularly the 12 empirical-test and 47 open-specification classifications. Do not infer novelty from catalogue placement or adjectives such as “derived.”
4. Review the historically proposed **superheavy stability limit**, semantic/entanglement claims, observer effects, and alleged new constants for possible additional **distinct** physical predictions. Split or merge NPL families only with a written rationale.
5. For every retained candidate, freeze a normalized mathematical statement, dimensional audit, theoretical comparator, discriminating experiment and decision gate. New letters and numerical simulation alone do not meet the standard.
6. Cross-check with the established physics literature for genuine novelty and prior-art equivalence before making priority claims.
7. Do not publicize a portfolio-wide *exact* candidate count until the all-item screening and duplicate reconciliation is completed.
